# Hello World

*June 2026*

This is my first blog post. Welcome to my corner of the internet.

I've been thinking a lot lately about how to share the things I'm working on — projects, ideas, experiments — without the overhead of a polished platform. This site is the answer.

## What to Expect

Expect posts about whatever I'm currently building or reading. No fixed schedule, no theme. Just notes made public.

## Why Markdown?

I write everything in Obsidian. It made sense to just... publish from there directly. This whole site is powered by raw `.md` files synced via Git. No CMS, no database.
